Social Audio Layer

Tuning Music

"No keyboard. Just music." — Turning songs into conversational currency.

The Friction: Streaming music is emotionally resonant yet completely anti-social. Sharing a track link on WhatsApp dumps recipients into an external app, losing the exact 15-second lyric moment that sparked the feeling.

The Innovation: Tuning turns song timestamps and lyrics into the messaging layer itself. Users share pinned lyric moments, react with contextual emojis, and chat without ever pausing playback.

Product Rigor: Normalized relational SQL schema (Users, Songs, TuningLogs) + freemium conversion model projecting +28% DAU lift and +45% session length.

Consumer Streaming Engine

Flux

Zero-latency Video ⇄ Audio switching with fair creator economics.

The Friction: Consumers juggle video (YouTube) and audio (Spotify/Podcasts) across siloed platforms. When commuting or multitasking with the screen off, video players force unnecessary screen rendering, wasting up to 75% mobile data and battery.

The Innovation: A unified playback engine that switches between video and background audio instantly without losing millisecond playback state.

Algorithmic Monetization: Features the "Active Audience Framework" and depth-weighted Creator Fair Pay algorithm, replacing impression vanity metrics to project a +22% 30-day retention lift and +35% creator earnings.

Enterprise FinTech Flagship

OneBank

Enterprise Core Banking Solution & Request Tracker

The Ground Bottleneck (DBS Context): In retail branch banking operations, customer service requests frequently fail because desk handovers occur across fragmented paper slips and siloed software. When an assigned officer is off-duty, customers are told "come back tomorrow," triggering severe SLA breaches, customer churn, and audit penalties.

Flagship Innovation: OneBank replaces handover amnesia with an immutable, branch-wide Request Tracker featuring real-time SLA aging clocks, instant cross-desk reassignment, and audit trails — reducing customer follow-up leakage to 0%.

Enterprise Modules: Engineered a 7-step digital e-KYC digital onboarding pipeline (Aadhaar, PAN, video verification, risk profiling), sub-second universal CBS account search across 7 tiers, and live FD/RD compounding and loan calculators in React 19 and TypeScript.
