OneBank — Enterprise Core Banking Solution & Request Tracker

Solving Retail Banking's Chronic "Come Back Tomorrow" Failure Loop

The Ground-Floor Problem (DBS Context)

In branch banking operations, customer service requests frequently fail because desk handovers occur across fragmented paper slips and siloed software. When an assigned officer is off-duty, customers are told "come back tomorrow," triggering severe SLA breaches, customer churn, and audit penalties.

Flagship Innovation: Unified Request Tracker

Designed an immutable, branch-wide Request Tracker with strict SLA aging, instant ownership reassignment, and audit trails. Table-stakes operations (7-step e-KYC onboarding, Deposits, Loan origination, CBS Search) are unified into a single coherent interface with zero external dependencies.

Technical Architecture
React 19 TypeScript Tailwind CSS Recharts Analytics DBS Domain Logic

Core Deliverables & Highlights

  • Flagship Request Tracker: Complete lifecycle tracking from intake to verification, dispatch, and closure with audit timestamps.
  • 7-Step e-KYC Onboarding: Aadhaar, PAN, Video verification, risk tiering, and instant customer ID generation.
  • Deposits & Loan Processing: Real-time FD/RD compounding calculations, loan stage pipeline, and EMI simulation.
  • Universal CBS Account Search: Sub-second customer lookup across 7 account categories with KYC validation states.

Flux — Unified Video & Audio Streaming Platform

Zero-Latency Mode Switching with Fair Creator Economics

The Context Fragmentation

Users consume music, podcasts, and video on separate platforms. When commuting or multitasking with the screen off, video players force unnecessary screen usage, draining up to 75% more mobile battery and mobile data bandwidth.

Flagship UX: Instant Video ⇄ Audio Toggle

Flux unifies both modes in a single playback pipeline. Users switch from video to background audio mode instantly without losing timestamp position, paired with an Active Audience Algorithm that compensates creators based on retention depth rather than surface impressions.

Stack & Media Pipeline
React TypeScript HTML5 Audio Engine State Persistence

Impact Modeling & Features

  • +22% Daily Retention Lift: Screen-off background playback eliminates session drops during commutes.
  • Active Audience Framework: Categorizes viewers into Active, Semi-Active, and Inactive based on interaction velocity.
  • +35% Creator Monetization: Depth-weighted CPM algorithm that rewards high-retention long-form creators.

How I Approach Product Strategy

Product management is not about opinions; it is about building tight feedback loops between qualitative customer friction and quantitative business economics.

Find the Unspoken Friction

Every feature must prove its economic viability: 100% IBM Excel certified modeling, SQL relational schemas, CAC/LTV payback, and retention funnel analytics.
