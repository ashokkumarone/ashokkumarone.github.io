Social Audio Layer

Tuning Music

"No keyboard. Just music." — Turning songs into conversational currency.

The Friction: Streaming music is emotionally resonant yet completely anti-social. Sharing a track link on WhatsApp dumps recipients into an external app, losing the exact 15-second lyric moment that sparked the feeling.

The Innovation: Tuning turns song timestamps and lyrics into the messaging layer itself. Users share pinned lyric moments, react with contextual emojis, and chat without ever pausing playback.

Product Rigor: Normalized relational SQL schema (Users, Songs, TuningLogs) + freemium conversion model projecting +28% DAU lift and +45% session length.

Consumer Streaming Engine

Flux

Zero-latency Video ⇄ Audio switching with fair creator economics.

The Friction: Consumers juggle video (YouTube) and audio (Spotify/Podcasts) across siloed platforms. When commuting or multitasking with the screen off, video players force unnecessary screen rendering, wasting up to 75% mobile data and battery.

The Innovation: A unified playback engine that switches between video and background audio instantly without losing millisecond playback state.

Algorithmic Monetization: Features the "Active Audience Framework" and depth-weighted Creator Fair Pay algorithm, replacing impression vanity metrics with watch-time depth to project a +22% 30-day retention lift and +35% creator earnings.
